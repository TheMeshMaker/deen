# Deen's name and icon

Deen's code is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later, with the additional terms in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). Its
**name and icon are not**: they tell people they are using the original app, published by its
author, with religious texts that have not been changed.

## What is protected

- The names **"Deen"** and **"دِين"** for an app, a website or a service in this area.
- The **Deen icon**: the letter nun (ن) drawn as a crescent, with a star for its dot, inside a thin
  eight-pointed star.
- The developer's name **"TheMeshMaker"**.

## What you may do

- Say truthfully that your work is "based on Deen" or "a fork of Deen", with a link to
  https://github.com/TheMeshMaker/deen.
- Write about Deen, review it, or link to it, using its name and icon to refer to the original app.
- Build Deen from source for yourself, unchanged, under its own name.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified version, or any app built from Deen's code, under the name Deen or دِين, a
  confusingly similar name, or with the Deen icon or something that looks like it.
- Suggest that a modified version is the original, or is made, published or endorsed by
  TheMeshMaker.
- Use the name or icon on a version whose Quran, hadith, adhkar, translations or sources have been
  changed.

A modified version must choose its own name and icon, and say in its description and its about screen
that it is a modified version of Deen.

## Reporting

The official Deen is published only by TheMeshMaker. To report a copy that uses Deen's name or icon,
or that hides where it came from, open an issue at https://github.com/TheMeshMaker/deen.
